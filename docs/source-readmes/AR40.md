# AR-40 — Product Analytics, Growth Intelligence & Continuous Optimization

Incremental pack over AR-39.

Implements a privacy-conscious product intelligence layer:
- first-party event contract and validation;
- session-scoped anonymous analytics identity;
- discovery/search/compare/offer/editorial funnels;
- retention cohorts without cross-site fingerprinting;
- product-quality and content-quality KPIs;
- experiment governance and exposure events;
- SEO/content opportunity projections;
- Admin intelligence dashboard;
- optimization review loop tied to user value.

Analytics is not allowed to weaken privacy, canonical governance, media rights,
security controls or editorial integrity.
