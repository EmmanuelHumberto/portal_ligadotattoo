# Final Product Principles

The portal should feel sophisticated, technically credible and visually distinct.

The dark premium interface, card-based information architecture and strong visual
hierarchy support discovery and longer useful sessions, but engagement is not
optimized as an end in itself.

The product prioritizes:
- trustworthy technical information;
- clear provenance;
- strong product discovery;
- useful comparisons;
- current commerce information;
- legitimate media usage;
- editorial depth;
- responsive performance;
- accessible navigation;
- operational reliability.

AI expands research and operational capability while remaining subordinate to
the platform's evidence and governance model.
