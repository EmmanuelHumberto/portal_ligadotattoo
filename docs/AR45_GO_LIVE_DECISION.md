# Final GO-Live Decision

Allowed states:

FORMULATION_COMPLETE
AR-01..AR-45 architecture/product formulation is complete.

IMPLEMENTATION_VERIFIED
AR-43 full integrated build/test evidence passes.

STAGING_VERIFIED
AR-44 + AR-38 staging evidence passes.

GO
Production launch is approved after all P0/P1 gates and operational/security
requirements pass.

CONDITIONAL_GO
Only non-critical deficiencies with owner, deadline, containment and explicit
approval. Cannot waive security, integrity, rights or authority controls.

NO_GO
Any blocking security/integrity/migration/authorization/rights/rollback issue.

Current AR-45 document may assert FORMULATION_COMPLETE. It must not assert
IMPLEMENTATION_VERIFIED, STAGING_VERIFIED or GO without execution evidence.
