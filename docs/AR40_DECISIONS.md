# AR-40 Decisions

1. Analytics is first-party and privacy-conscious.
2. Anonymous identity is session-scoped in the baseline.
3. Raw IP and raw search text are not product analytics dimensions.
4. Event names/properties use explicit allowlists.
5. Funnels measure discovery quality, not mandatory conversion.
6. Product-quality coverage is measured alongside engagement.
7. Experiments require hypotheses, metrics, guardrails and ownership.
8. Experiment assignment is deterministic per session.
9. Experiments cannot weaken provenance, rights, security or editorial integrity.
10. Content opportunity scoring combines demand and supply gaps.
11. Time-on-site is not treated as an unconditional success metric.
12. Optimization decisions are recorded and reversible.
